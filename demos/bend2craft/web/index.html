Graphics

Each slider is a share of the game's own look: 0% switches the effect off, 100% is how it ships and 200% doubles it. Graphics Quality decides what the machine can afford; these decide how much of each effect you want, and they hold whichever tier is running. The first four are the ones the tier prices per frame, so moving them moves the frame rate; the rest are already paid for and cost nothing to turn up.

How to Play

  • WASD move · Mouse look · Space jump · Shift sneak · Ctrl sprint
  • Left click mine or attack the mob under the crosshair · Right click place · F attack (bow selected: shoot)
  • Shield selected blocks 2/3 of mob damage · Swords deal 4–7 damage
  • 1–9 hotbar · E inventory · R furnace
  • G eat · T trade · N sleep · Esc pause
