# Open issues

Keep one ticket per `.scratch/<feature>/issues/NN-<slug>.md`, numbered from `01`.
Use `spec.md` in the feature directory only for requirements shared by its tickets.

Each ticket states the problem, acceptance criteria, blockers and a `Status:`:
`needs-triage`, `needs-info`, `ready-for-agent`, `ready-for-human` or `wontfix`.
Keep reproduction steps and external test oracles needed to finish the work.
Delete completed tickets and superseded specs; their evidence remains in Git.

When a skill says to publish to the issue tracker, use this layout for open work.
Research belongs in ignored `research/` under the policy in `AGENTS.md`.
